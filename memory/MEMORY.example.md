## Standing rules

- [Who I am](user_background.md), second-year master's student, coastal
  engineering group. Working hours, supervisor, what I am assessed on.
- [Say unverified out loud](feedback_no_unverified_claims.md), separate what
  was run from what is expected, in the same message. Enforced by the output
  rule table.
- [Plain language first](feedback_explain_plainly.md), do not lead with an
  internal name. Order: what breaks, what to look at, what the choice is.
- [Do not ask me to type data by hand](feedback_no_manual_data_entry.md), any
  measurement that depends on daily manual entry will stop within a week.
- [Cite the file, not your memory](feedback_cite_the_file.md), when a claim
  comes from a document, give the path and the line.
- [Decide, do not ask](feedback_decide_dont_ask.md), if the rules here settle
  it, act. Ask only when they genuinely do not.
